Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital Citizenship is the practice of using technology safely.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law is the set of rules and laws that govern how people use technology.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for an Acceptable Use Policy, which is a set of rules and guidelines that determine how a person can use a company or school's network.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's work, ideas, or words as your own without proper credit.

It doesn't matter where it's done on purpose or by accident-it's still plagiarism.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic Dishonesty is any action that gives a student an unfair advantage or misrepresents their learning.

In simple terms, it's cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

AI-generated content may be used only when allowed and only in responsible ways. AI is a tool to support learning-not a replacement for thinking or original work.
